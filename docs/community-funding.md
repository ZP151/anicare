# Community-first operating and funding model

## Decision

Core browsing, reporting, identity review, follows and safety features remain free. Paying cannot increase visibility, influence identity, accelerate moderation or unlock exact locations. This avoids turning community truth or animal safety into a purchasable advantage.

For the MVP, do not accept user-to-user money and do not host individual fundraising campaigns. These create beneficiary verification, fraud, disputes, refunds, accounting and regulatory obligations before the product has proven its core value.

## Funding sequence

1. Founder funding and cloud credits for the alpha.
2. Grants or sponsorships with published conflict rules and no access to user/location data.
3. A written partnership with a Singapore registered charity/IPC for any named animal-welfare appeal; funds should go directly to that beneficiary where practical.
4. Only after legal/accounting review, optional unrestricted contributions toward platform operations with transparent quarterly use-of-funds reporting.

If the organisation is not an approved IPC, never market contributions as tax-deductible. IRAS states that tax-deductible cash donations are generally donations to approved IPCs for local causes, and that material benefits can change the treatment: [IRAS donations and tax deductions](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/other-taxes/charities/donations-tax-deductions).

## Donation interaction rules

- Donation is never a condition for posting, matching, reviewing, following or receiving a welfare update.
- No dark patterns, public donor ranking, streaks or emotional pressure tied to an individual cat.
- State the legal recipient, beneficiary, purpose, fees, refund position and whether tax relief applies before payment.
- Keep donation and disbursement records for at least five years and use funds only for the stated purpose, following the Commissioner of Charities guidance: [fund-raiser duties and obligations](https://www.charities.gov.sg/Pages/Fund-Raising/Fund-Raisers-Duties-and-Obligations.aspx).
- Separate sponsorship from donation. Sponsor acknowledgement cannot confer governance or data privileges.
- Publish aggregate receipts, payment fees, programme spend and platform operating spend quarterly.

## Sustainability metrics

- Monthly infrastructure and moderation cost per weekly contributing caregiver.
- Months of unrestricted runway.
- Percentage of spending on moderation/safety, infrastructure and direct programme support.
- Donor concentration and sponsor conflict exposure.
- Community value metrics from the product charter; never optimise donation conversion as the primary product KPI.
